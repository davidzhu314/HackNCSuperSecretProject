# Landing page (`/tour`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: real profile-polishers and HackNC judges, equally. Action: open the studio (`/studio`), with sample mode one click in. Proof: the fictitious demo persona's real sample critiques and the intentional "95% at Google" FactCheck test case, labeled sample. Constraint: studio keeps its incumbent Aero look for now; landing alone adopts the Avoriaz-derived world (user-pinned, see AVORIAZ_STYLE_GUIDE.md).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The profile is the panorama. The user's own sentences fill the screen and simulated critiques pin to them as hotspots, the way a 360° tour pins places. Refuses the SaaS split hero and the three-card feature row.
OWN-WORLD: Deep navy #2A354B and black grounds, apricot #FFB36D as the single committed accent (fields, hotspots, buttons), slate #57617A text on white sheets. Extra-condensed uppercase display (Archivo at wdth 62, stand-in for Founders Grotesk X-Condensed), outlined hero lettering with an apricot clip-path wipe. Square corners or full circles only; no shadows, no cards. Navy text on apricot (contrast fix of the source).
STORY: Visitors see a bland profile critiqued sentence by sentence, see a fabricated rewrite get caught, understand only confirmed facts count, then open the studio.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. Top: wordmark left, LinkedIn/Hinge pill switch, square apricot "Open studio" right. Center: the demo profile's segments set large as outlined condensed caps, apricot hotspot circles on s1-s4 that open slate-on-white pills with audience, rating, rationale. Bottom-left info block: 40px title, 18px line, apricot CTA "Start with your profile". Sample label visible.
FORM: Brief-pinned (Avoriaz virtual tour grammar); no concept-seed roll. Seed key: none (user-pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
